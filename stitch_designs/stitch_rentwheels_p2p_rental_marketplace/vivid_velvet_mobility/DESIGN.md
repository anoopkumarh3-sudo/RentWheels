## Brand & Style
The design system drives a forward-looking, high-end peer-to-peer vehicle marketplace. It blends high-performance automotive precision with contemporary luxury hospitality. The interface evokes executive confidence, exhilarating velocity, and effortless mobility.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with **Modern Tactile Precision** accented by vibrant, deep-spectrum lighting:
- **Luminous Violet Accents**: Vivid electric purple operates as the primary catalyst for conversion, active states, and premium badges, injecting vital digital energy without visual clutter.
- **Architectural Slate & Crisp Porcelain**: Clean porcelain light slate surfaces maintain breathing room, allowing photographic vehicle assets and mechanical data to command focus.
- **Midnight Plum Anchors**: Rich, atmospheric deep plum neutrals replace clinical blacks, delivering commanding editorial contrast and luxury depth.

## Colors
The palette balances vibrant electric luminescence with executive midnight tones and pristine neutral surfaces:

- **Primary (`#7C3AED`)**: Electric violet delivers focused visual weight for core interaction points, primary booking calls to action, search execution buttons, and active vehicle telemetry pins.
- **Secondary (`#DDD6FE`)**: Soft lilac tint designed for tonal chips, soft feature highlights, and pill backdrops. Pair with `#EDE9FE` for low-contrast section washes.
- **Tertiary (`#1E1035`)**: Deep midnight plum used for display headlines, dark-mode hero treatments, elevated banners, and luxury tier accents.
- **Neutral (`#0F0E17`)**: High-contrast obsidian slate grounding all typographic hierarchy and key structural frames.
- **Surfaces & Borders**: Layered `#F8FAFC` base canvas with crisp `#FFFFFF` cards, framed by subtle purple-tinted hairline borders (`#E9E5F5`) to tie the ambient palette into the grid architecture.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves across headlines, body copy, and UI metadata, unifying modern geometric authority with approachable clarity:

- **Headlines & Display**: Rendered in dark midnight plum (`#1E1035`) to reinforce luxury stance and bold automotive posture.
- **Body & Specifications**: Set in medium and light weights with generous leading for dense spec tables, vehicle history records, and trip itineraries.
- **Tabular Figures**: Force `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` across all rates, hourly trip timers, odometer readings, and host earnings metrics to prevent horizontal layout shift during dynamic updates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive structural grid with controlled breakpoints:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `4rem` outer canvas margins. Maximizes vehicle split layouts, map exploration rails, and side-by-side spec sheets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` outer margins. Filter panels tuck into floating slide-out drawer sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` margins. Primary checkout and booking controls dock permanently to the bottom viewport edge.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed by an 8px base grid. Component interior spacing utilizes `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while layout sections transition via `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through soft, tinted ambient shadows with subtle violet diffraction rather than uncolored black drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Pure `#F8FAFC` slate canvas.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` card face with `1px solid #E9E5F5` border and a purple-tinted ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 16, 53, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns)**: Interactive vehicle cards and floating search modules lift on hover: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(30, 16, 53, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Trays & Modals)**: Bottom sheets and checkout bars use `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 16, 53, 0.16)` paired with a frosted glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over an 85% opacity `#FFFFFF` background).

## Shapes
A disciplined 8px (`0.5rem`) default radius anchors interactive stability while selective pill curves provide athletic contrast:

- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Field Sets)**: Strictly 8px (`0.5rem`) for crisp mechanical precision.
- **Surface Cards & Modals**: 16px (`1rem`) corner radius for content modules, vehicle cards, and image viewports.
- **Badges, Filter Chips, & Live Tags**: Pill radius (`9999px`) creates rapid visual differentiation against structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#7C3AED`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, height `48px`, border-radius `8px`. Hover shifts to `#6D28D9` with a subtle purple-tinted glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.35)`). Active click states scale to `0.98`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background, border `1px solid #DDD6FE`, text `#1E1035`. Hover transitions background to `#EDE9FE`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, text `#7C3AED`, padding inline `0.75rem`. Hover applies `#EDE9FE` background.

### Vehicle Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `1px solid #E9E5F5` border framing and 16px border-radius.
- Aspect-ratio 16:10 vehicle media hero with a top-left pill overlay (`#1E1035` with 80% opacity and `#EDE9FE` text) for trip tags like "Instant Confirmation" or "Superhost".
- Host rating and daily price highlights anchored cleanly using tabular typography at the card footer.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default / Unselected**: `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F0E17`, height `36px`, rounded `9999px`.
- **Active / Selected**: `#EDE9FE` subtle lavender background, `1.5px solid #7C3AED`, text `#7C3AED` with `label-md` font weight.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Resting state: `48px` height, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F0E17`, border-radius `8px`.
- Focus state: Border sharpens to `#7C3AED` with a `3px` focus ring in `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`. Floating labels render in `#7C3AED`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Selected state: `#7C3AED` fill with `#FFFFFF` glyph.
- Unselected state: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` with `#FFFFFF` interior.

### Status & Verification Badges
- **Verified Host Badge**: `#EDE9FE` lavender background with `#7C3AED` icon and text label.
- **Electric Vehicle / Instant Lock**: Crisp electric violet pill with glowing neon dot indicator (`#8B5CF6`).
